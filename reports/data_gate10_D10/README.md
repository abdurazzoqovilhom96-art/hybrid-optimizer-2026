## Why this is not the result

**Five runs, not the competition's 51.** Nothing here can carry a p-value: with
5 runs the paired Wilcoxon signed-rank test cannot go below 2/2^5 = 0.0625,
which fails any correction. The ranks are a direction to test, not a finding.

**Recorded under seed scheme v1.** The driver then seeded each algorithm from
its position in `sorted(ALL_ALGORITHMS)`, so removing the weak swarm baselines
re-seeded every remaining algorithm. These rows therefore **cannot be
reproduced** against the current code, and `run_all.py --resume` refuses to
append to them. See `algorithm_seed` in `temoa/registry.py`.

**Old line-up.** Eight algorithms including TEMOA_V10/V11 under their old names;
`L-SHADE-DGR` did not exist yet.

The convergence curves (`curves_10D.npz`, 516 KB) were not kept: nothing cites
them, and they are regenerated by any re-run.

Replaced by the E1 gate: 29 functions, **51 runs**, 100 000 evaluations, the
current eight-rival line-up, seed scheme v2.
